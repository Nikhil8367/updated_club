Inauguration

The grand beginning of the GVP Data Science Club.

Event Date

24 NOV 2022

Event Overview

The Data Science Club at Gayatri Vidya Parishad College of Engineering held its grand Inauguration Ceremony on November 24, 2022. The event marked the official launch of the club, with enthusiastic participation from students and faculty. The ceremony highlighted the club’s vision and mission to advance knowledge in Data Science and technology. It concluded with a group photograph, symbolizing unity and excitement for the club’s future events and activities.

Key Takeaways

  • 📌 Explore the world of Data Science
  • 📌 Engage in fun activities
