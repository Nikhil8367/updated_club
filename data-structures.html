DSA Mastery

Deep dive into data structures and their algorithmic complexities with hands-on labs.

Event Date

28 DEC 2024

Event Overview

To conclude the year on a high note, the club organized the DATASTRUCT Workshop, which focused on one of the most critical areas of computer science: data structures and algorithms. This workshop aimed to strengthen students’ problem-solving capabilities and enhance their understanding of algorithmic efficiency. The session covered a wide array of topics, including arrays, linked lists, trees, graphs, and sorting algorithms. Students were guided through hands-on coding exercises that demonstrated how to apply these concepts to real-world scenarios. This workshop not only prepared participants for technical interviews but also laid a strong foundation for their future endeavors in software engineering and data science.

Key Takeaways

  • 📌 Revisit and strengthen DSA fundamentals
  • 📌 Enhance coding and problem-solving skills
